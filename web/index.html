        About WheelGrade

        WheelGrade grades cash-secured puts and covered calls against a fixed set of rules — Elite quality first, rent of at least ~1%/month, a ~30Δ strike, no earnings print inside the expiry, a basis you'd be glad to own, and a spread tight enough to fill — then tracks the premium you actually collect.
